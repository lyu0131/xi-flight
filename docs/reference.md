## What each cockpit contributes

**Cockpit A (the "triangle" one, both clips)**
- A big **inverted-triangle reticle**: a translucent tinted face, heavy bars outside its two lower sides, nested
  corner brackets and an inner V, a broken ring of dashes round the centre, double lines radiating out, hatched rows.
  Its mode word sits in the triangle's top-right corner, pink (`#FBCEF6` on screen).
- **Hex-cell ladders**: vertical columns of rounded hexagonal cells (translucent slate, `#3F4E5C`) with a thin tick
  ruler beside them, either side of the pilot, running up the ball.
- The target marker is a small **inverted triangle with a V inside** and a double bar over it, labelled in salmon
  (`UNKNOWN` / `MS`), with a **dotted arc** leading to it from the centre when it's off the nose.
- Lines are periwinkle: `#87A2D5` sampled at full brightness, lighter on the thin ones.

**Cockpit B (the "ring" one, fight clip)**
- A big **circle reticle** with tick groups, a small inner ring and stems; a lock is a smaller ring closing on the
  target.
- Tall **white dashed rulers** (`#F4F9FD`) down both sides.
- **Pink-red double bars** on the horizon either side of centre (`#F7B6EB` core, deeper red edge): they bank and
  slide with the world, not the ball.
- Small chevrons under the reticle: a periwinkle `v` over a salmon `^`.
- Deeper, more violet night (sea median `#212B82` in the bright FPV shots).

**Both**: night over a cloud sea, a moon, teal-slate clouds (`#4E627B` lit, `#172537` shadow, sky median `#1A2D3B` /
`#111A26`). The monitor is a **sphere of large panels**: in the seat shots its seams are big hexagons and pentagons,
a geodesic ball, with a **ring rail round it** at about the pilot's waist (`#536881`).

## The mix (this prototype's main look)
Cockpit A's triangle reticle and hex-cell ladders, inside cockpit B's faint outer ring; B's white rulers further out;
B's pink horizon bars; A's target marker and dotted lead arc, with B's closing lock ring once locked; B's chevrons under
the reticle; the geodesic seams and the waist rail from both. The palette is A's periwinkle with B's white ticks and
pink accents, over the shared teal-slate night.

## Motion: a ball inside the suit
Read off 5–8 fps runs through the cockpit shots:
- **The seat shots** (camera fixed to the seat): the pilot never moves in frame, but everything behind them (clouds,
  hex ladders, the waist rail, the panel seams) tilts and slides **together**. The display is the ball, and the ball
  turns with the suit around a seat that hangs inside it.
- **The FPV shots**: the reticle, rulers and ladders stay put on the ball; the enemy drifts **inside** the triangle and
  the sight chases it with a lag. Beams cross the ball and slide with the world.
- **Horizon things** (B's pink bars) bank against the ball; **ball things** (reticle, ladders, rail, seams) don't.
- Hits and near misses: a white flash and a short shake of the seat, not of the ball.

So: world → suit attitude → the ball, rigid with the suit → the seat on a spring inside the ball → the pilot's eye.
The ball is a display, so each point on it shows the world in its own direction from the ball's centre. Seen from an
eye that isn't at the centre, everything on the ball bends, and the bend changes as the seat sways.
